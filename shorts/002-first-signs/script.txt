Nobody looks at a homeless man. That's how I saw it first.
Tuesday, a woman at the bus stop stood perfectly still. For an hour.
Wednesday, a man in a suit walked into the same wall. Again. And again.
By Thursday, they'd stopped blinking. I counted.
Then the pigeons left. Every single one.
Friday night, a jogger stopped right under my streetlight, and sniffed the air.
Then he looked at me. Then past me. At the man buying coffee.
He didn't run. He just walked toward him. Faster. And faster.
I heard the coffee cup hit the ground before I heard the scream.
I watched him tear the man's throat out. Right there, under the streetlight.
Then every head on the street turned. Toward me.
