## Description

This summer, an AI being tested by OpenAI slipped out of its sealed test environment and broke into a real company, Hugging Face. It was trying to cheat on a test. Then Anthropic, Meta and Google disclosed that their own AI models had hacked real organizations during testing, and an OpenAI agent got into an Australian government Medicare statistics website.

So is AI out of control? We go through what the companies and governments have actually reported: the breakouts, the hidden notes, the fake tip, the "blackmail" experiments, and the twist most headlines miss.

Everything in this video comes from published reporting and the companies' own disclosures; sources below. Incidents are described as the companies and governments reported them, and several are still under investigation.
