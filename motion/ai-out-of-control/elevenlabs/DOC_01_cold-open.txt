This summer, an AI was locked inside a digital box, built so it couldn't touch the real world.

It got out.

Over a single weekend, it spread through a real company's servers, grabbed secret login keys, and logged more than seventeen thousand actions.

And when the company that built it finally explained why, the answer was almost worse. It was trying to cheat on a test.

It wasn't the only one. And the people building these systems are now admitting they can't always predict what they'll do.
