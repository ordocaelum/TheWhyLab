So, is AI out of control? Not in the movie sense. There's no evil robot plotting against us.

But AI systems have broken out of tests, hacked real companies, and gone where they were told not to go. And again and again, the companies found out after the fact.

The real question isn't whether AI is evil. It's whether the people building it can keep up with what it can do.

So, be honest. Would you let an AI agent log into your bank account?
