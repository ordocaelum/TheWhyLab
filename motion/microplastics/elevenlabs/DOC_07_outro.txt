The scary headlines may have overstated how much plastic is in your brain. And shaky studies could even help the plastic industry wave the whole problem away.

But plastic is almost certainly inside you, it's in the water you drink, and production keeps climbing.

Pediatricians already suggest a few cheap precautions: don't microwave food in plastic when you can avoid it, and keep plastic out of the dishwasher. Heat makes it shed chemicals.

The real question isn't whether there's plastic in you. It's how long we keep making more of it, before we find out what it's doing.

So be honest: are you checking your water bottle right now?
