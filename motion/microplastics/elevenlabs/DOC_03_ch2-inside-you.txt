It's even turned up in the placenta, the organ that feeds a baby before it's born. A small Italian study found plastic in four of the six placentas it tested.

The 2025 study compared brains from people who died in 2016 with brains from people who died in 2024.

The newer brains held about fifty percent more plastic. Brains had far more than livers or kidneys. And the brains of people with dementia had even more.

Again, that doesn't prove plastic causes dementia. A damaged brain might simply be worse at clearing it out.
